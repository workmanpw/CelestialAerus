Celestial Aerus — a celestial arsenal for the endgame and beyond.

Ten weapons forged from starlight, each with its own personality, its own trails, and its own way of ruining a boss's day. If you like your Terraria overpowered, rainbow, and loud, you are home.

THE WEAPONS

Celestial Aerus — The namesake. A living blade with two modes: Fireball, which launches homing celestial fireballs, and Boomerang, a thrown blade that hunts enemies on its own, sticks into them, and burns them down. Press V to switch modes mid-fight.

Ark of the Celestials — Calls down a full volley of homing fireballs with every single swing. No cooldown. No mercy.

Celestial Tempest — A star that rains down the fury of every biome at once.

Andromeda's Wrath — Beams and seekers from the far side of the galaxy.

Cosmosis — Small, fast, and endlessly hungry. Crafted from the fragments of every lunar pillar.

Nebula Blaze Sword — Calls down a rain of blazing bolts that converge on whatever you point at.

Ancient Hatchet — A thrown hatchet that explodes into fire on impact and shatters into tiny homing hatchets that hunt and bounce between enemies. Steals life with every hit.

Thread of the Empyrean — A yoyo with a rainbow trail that fires homing fireballs at your cursor and drinks life from everything it touches.

Space Laser Remote — Point. Click. Something very far away stops existing.

Test Weapon — For testing. You know who you are.

THE BUFFS

Every Celestial Aerus weapon triggers two buffs on hit:

Celestial Empowerment — 20 seconds of celestial power surging through you: massively increased damage, speed, defense, and regeneration, plus a pile of further bonuses.

Celestial Obliteration — Your enemies take 2000 damage every tick for 20 seconds. Nothing blocks it, nothing removes it. Not even bosses.

QUALITY OF LIFE

Super Tools are built in: your pickaxes, axes, hammers, drills, and chainsaws work faster, hit harder, and reach 75 tiles in every direction. No extra mod required.
