BRIGITTE'S CHARM - IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
Updated: 2026-11-16

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

- The charm builds a cached pool of eligible vanilla weapon and ammo shots.
  It excludes hooks, spears, flails, lances, explosive projectiles, biome
  solutions and throwables, sandgun shots, and non-weapon effects.
- Storm shots retain their vanilla projectile type and texture but use the
  charm's shared radial flight and homing behavior. Their vanilla AI and tile
  collision are disabled so missing weapon AI arguments cannot stop them or
  change terrain.
- Storm volleys continue every 30 ticks near enemies and every 60 ticks
  otherwise, with an active cap of 60 and a 180-tick lifetime.
- Storm and cascade projectiles use 20-tick local NPC immunity. Shots from
  charm-summoned minions and sentries get local immunity through their parent
  projectile; ordinary minions keep vanilla immunity.
- Vanilla and mod weapon minions and vanilla sentries are maintained while
  the charm is equipped. Sentries follow the owner and retain their sentry AI.

BUG FOUND AND FIXED

- The latest client log showed `KeyNotFoundException` for
  `CharmGlobalProjectile` from `SpawnMinion`, `RefreshMinionTimeLeft`,
  `FireStormVolley`, `CountActiveStormProjectiles`, and `TryCascade`.
- tModLoader calls `Projectile.SetDefaults(type)` inside `NewProjectile`
  before it assigns the projectile owner or final friendly state. The global
  projectile filter depended on those fields, so charm projectiles did not
  receive the global instance and the charm logic failed when retrieving it.
- The filter now selects by projectile type: charm storm/cascade/minion/sentry
  types and vanilla minion/sentry child shots are covered before owner setup.

VALIDATION

- `dotnet build` succeeded with zero warnings and zero errors.
- An isolated dedicated-server smoke test loaded CelestialAerus and reached
  `Server started` using a temporary test world; the temporary files were
  removed afterward.
- Live player combat has not yet been tested after the attachment fix. Confirm
  tModLoader reloads the rebuilt mod, then check that minions appear and the
  storm fires in-game.
